## ✅ Answer: They're Almost The Same

**The 2020-2024 period did NOT have significantly more trades!**

### Breakdown:
- **428 trades** in 2016-2020 = **85.6 per year**
- **446 trades** in 2020-2024 = **89.3 per year**
- **Difference: +4.3%** (18 more trades over 5 years)

This small increase is explained by:

1. **Slightly more TP exits** (41% vs 39.5%)
   - TP exits close faster than SL exits
   - Average SL exit: 51h vs 41h
   - Faster exits = slightly more turnover

2. **Slightly more structure breaks** (+2.6%)
   - More OB + structure alignments
   - A few more valid entry signals

3. **Shorter average duration** (-7.9%)
   - Trades closed 4 hours faster on average
   - More capital turnover

## 🤔 What You Might Have Been Confused By

### The RETURNS Were Dramatically Different

| Period | Trades | Return | Annual CAGR |
|--------|--------|--------|-------------|
| 2016-2020 | 428 | +823% | +56% |
| 2020-2024 | 446 | **+128,624%** | **+319%** |

**Same number of trades, but 5.7× higher CAGR!**

### Why Such Different Returns With Similar Trade Counts?

**1. Risk Per Trade:**
- 2016-2020: **1% risk** per trade
- 2020-2024: **3% risk** per trade (3× larger positions)

**2. Compound Growth Effect:**

With 3% risk:
- Each win adds 3× more capital
- Each loss takes 3× more capital
- But with 41% win rate + 2:1 R/R + 2× profit factor = **NET POSITIVE**
- This compounds EXPONENTIALLY

**Example:**
```
1% risk strategy:
$1,000 → $1,030 → $1,061 → $1,093 (slow)

3% risk strategy:
$1,000 → $1,090 → $1,188 → $1,295 (fast)

After 100 trades with same win rate:
1% risk: ~$3,000
3% risk: ~$50,000
```

**3. Winner Size in 2020-2024:**

The 2020-2024 period had:
- Best single trade: **+$102,227**
- Worst single trade: -$38,316
- Average trade: **$2,884**

Compare to 2016-2020:
- Best single trade: +$272
- Worst single trade: -$99
- Average trade: $19

**Why?** Because by late 2024, the account had grown to $1.29M, so 3% risk = **MASSIVE position sizes**.

## 💡 The Real Insight

### It's Not About Trade Frequency

**Both periods had ~86-89 trades per year** (very consistent)

### It's About Position Size + Compound Growth

**2020-2024 was exceptional because:**

1. **3% risk** = 3× larger positions than 1% risk
2. **Compound growth** = exponential gains as account grew
3. **Late-stage trades** were HUGE (by 2024, 3% of $1M = $30k positions!)

### Trade Size Evolution (2020-2024 example):

```
Early 2020 (account = $1,000):
  3% risk = $30 position

  3% risk = $1,500 position
```

**Same number of trades, but position sizes grew 1,000×!**
