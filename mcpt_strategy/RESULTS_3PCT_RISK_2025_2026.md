## ⚠️ Risk Analysis

### Why No Margin Call Despite Large Drawdown?

**1. Adaptive Position Sizing**
```
At $1,000: Risk $30/trade → 0.30 lots
At $600:   Risk $18/trade → 0.18 lots (40% smaller)

Position size scales DOWN as equity falls ✅
```

**2. Conservative Leverage Usage**
```
Even at worst:
- Equity: $597
- Position: 0.18 lots
- Value: ~$19,000
- Leverage: 18.82:1
- Required Margin: $380 (at 50:1)
- Margin Level: 157% (safe) ✅
```

**3. ATR-Based Stops**
```
Stop distances adapt to volatility
Larger ATR = wider stops = smaller positions
Automatic leverage control ✅
```

### When Would Margin Call Happen?

**Required Conditions:**
```
1. Equity drops to $400-500
2. AND simultaneously take maximum position
3. AND volatility is low (tight stops = bigger positions)

Probability: LOW
Reason: Position sizing prevents this scenario
```
