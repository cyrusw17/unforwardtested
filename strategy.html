Volatility regime

ATR z-score (50-bar) scales stops and targets so risk adapts across COVID, hike, and chop regimes.

  • High vol (z > 1.5) Multiply SL/TP by 0.75 — tighter targets
  • Normal Standard ATR multiples
  • Low vol (z < −1.0) Multiply SL/TP by 1.25 — more room
  • Trend boost If ADX > 30, take-profit × 1.5
